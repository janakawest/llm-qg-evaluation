# Evaluation Rubric for MCQ Generation

| **Criteria**                          | **2 (Good)**                                                                                     | **1 (Fair)**                                                                                     | **0 (Poor)**                                                                                     |
|---------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1. Relevance to Provided Context**  | The question directly relates to the key concepts, terms, or examples from the given material. | Somewhat related but misses important details or includes minor off-topic information. | The question doesn’t fit the given context and brings in unrelated or incorrect concepts. |
| **2. Alignment with Learning Outcomes** | Clearly tests a skill or knowledge area outlined in the learning objectives. | Loosely connected to the learning outcomes but doesn’t fully measure what it should. | Doesn’t align with any stated learning objective or tests an unrelated concept. |
| **3. Quality of Distractors**  | The incorrect answer choices (distractors) are realistic and reflect common mistakes learners might make. | Some distractors are either too obvious, too vague, or not realistic enough to be effective. | The distractors don’t make sense, are repetitive, or are so obviously incorrect that they don’t challenge the learner. |
| **4. Clarity and Precision** | The question is clear, well-worded, and free of errors. There’s only one correct answer, and it’s easy to understand. | Some minor wording issues or typos, but the question is still answerable. | The question is confusing, contains errors, or could lead to multiple correct answers. |
| **5. Ambiguity** *(New Criterion)* | The meaning of the question and answer choices is crystal clear. | There’s a slight chance of misinterpretation, but most learners would still understand it. | The question is vague or misleading, making it unclear what the correct answer is. |
| **6. Format Consistency** *(New Criterion)* | The structure, phrasing, and formatting follow a consistent style, making the question easy to read. | There are small inconsistencies in formatting or wording, but they don’t make the question hard to follow. | The question is poorly structured or formatted inconsistently, making it hard to understand. |
| **7. Cognitive Level Alignment** *(New Criterion, replacing Difficulty Level)* | The question is designed at the right level—whether it’s testing recall, application, or higher-order thinking—based on the learning goals. | The question somewhat fits the intended cognitive level but may be slightly too easy or too difficult. | The question doesn’t match the expected level of thinking and isn’t appropriate for the assessment’s purpose. |
